Install CORAL on Mobile

No Google Play Store Required!

CORAL is built as a Progressive Web Application (PWA). You can install it directly from your browser to your home screen for instant offline access in Corcuera.

Installation Steps:

Android (Chrome / Brave)

  1. Tap browser menu at top right.
  2. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  3. Confirm prompt to launch as native app.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap Share icon in lower toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap Add at top right.
